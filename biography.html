Photographer Dossier

Dominik Šuškevič

Lithuanian visual artist, photographer, and director exploring the intersection of atmosphere, natural light, and human emotion.

Path Through the Lens

Born and working in Lithuania, Dominik Šuškevič began documenting natural landscapes, historic estates, and urban life with a focus on quiet stillness and honest light.

Over the years, his practice expanded from still photography across Švenčionys, Vilnius, and Kaunas into architectural heritage, equestrian documentary work, automotive events, and independent narrative cinema.
