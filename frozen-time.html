Cinematography Project

Frozen Time: What If

A visual poem asking how a single suspended moment reshapes memory, choice, and silence.

Synopsis & Production

“Frozen Time: What If” translates the still-frame language of DP.PORTFOLIO into motion picture. Shot across quiet Lithuanian landscapes and architectural interiors, the film slows down perception so every glance, shadow, and breath carries dramatic weight.

Directed, photographed, and graded by Dominik Šuškevič under DP | CORPORATION, the project explores minimal dialogue paired with deliberate framing and atmospheric sound design.
