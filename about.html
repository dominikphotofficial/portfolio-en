About DP.PORTFOLIO

A calm visual archive dedicated to space, natural light, and authentic human presence across Lithuania and the Baltics.

DP | CORPORATION

DP.PORTFOLIO is the creative studio and editorial archive founded by photographer and filmmaker Dominik Šuškevič. Every series begins with observation — allowing light, weather, and architectural geometry to set the rhythm before a single shutter is pressed.

From quiet wildlife studies in Lithuanian oak forests and manor portraits to high-energy automotive gatherings in Kaunas, the portfolio preserves each story in clean, unhurried compositions without artificial distraction.

Operating under DP | CORPORATION, we collaborate with private clients, cultural institutions, equestrian stables, and independent film festivals to craft timeless imagery and short-form cinema.
